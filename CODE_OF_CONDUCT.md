# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment. Be
constructive, criticize evidence and code rather than people, disclose conflicts of
interest, protect confidential material, and correct errors transparently.

Maintainers may remove abusive, deceptive, unsafe, or privacy-violating contributions
and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or research
record. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
Good-faith scientific disagreement is welcome; intimidation, discrimination,
retaliation, doxxing, and fabricated or laundered evidence are not.
